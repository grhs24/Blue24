Foothold Now

Privacy

What you practice is personal. Foothold Now is built so that none of it leaves your device unless you ask it to.

The short version: your ladders live in your browser. Signing in is optional and exists for one reason—so your ladders can follow you between your phone and your computer. Stay signed out and nothing you write leaves this device. Either way, we don’t sell your data, share it, or build profiles.

What stays on your device

Everything, always. Your ladders, steps, ratings, notes, and practice logs are stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device, and that copy is the one the app reads and writes as you work. It keeps working with no connection at all. The Backup button gives you your data as a file whenever you want it, so you can keep a copy or move it yourself.

If you never sign in

Then nothing you write is sent anywhere, at all. Signed out, the app makes no request of any kind to any server—not your ladder text, not your ratings, not your notes. That is how the app opens by default, and you never have to leave it.

If you choose to sign in

Signing in exists so your ladders can travel between your devices. You give an email address, we email you a one-time code, and typing that code in signs you in. There is no password to choose or remember.

Once you are signed in, two things are kept away from your device:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in.
  • A copy of your ladders, so that another device you sign in on can pick them up.

That copy sits in a database row tied to your account and protected by row-level security, which means no other account can read it. The database is hosted for us by Supabase. Your ladders are not read for any other purpose, not analyzed, and never sold or shared. Signing out on a device unlinks that device from the account, and the ladders already on it stay exactly where they are.

What we don’t collect

No names, no profiles, no advertising trackers, and no cookies. Your ladders are never read for any purpose beyond showing them back to you. The one thing counted is how many times the website is opened—an anonymous tally that sets no cookies, records nothing that identifies you, and cannot see a single word you write.

Deleting your data

The copy on your device is yours to clear anytime—remove the site’s data in your browser settings, or clear your browser storage, and it’s gone.

If you have signed in, the Account panel has Delete my synced copy, which erases the synced copy of your ladders from the server and signs the device out, leaving the ladders on your device untouched. To have the email address behind the account removed as well, write to footholdnowsupport@gmail.com and it will be deleted.
