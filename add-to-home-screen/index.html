Add Foothold Now to your phone’s home screen

Foothold Now is a website, so there’s nothing to install and no app store to visit—but your phone can still give it a place on the home screen, right next to your apps. It takes about a minute.

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open footholdnow.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button—the square with the arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name or shorten it, then tap Add.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Open footholdnow.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install).
  4. Confirm with Add.

Either way, the Foothold Now icon lands on your home screen, and tapping it opens the site in your browser—with everything you’ve already built waiting for you, since your ladders live safely in that same browser.

Two small tips: your ladders stay on the device they were made on unless you sign in with your email (the Sign in button, top right), which keeps them in sync across your phone and computer. And the Backup button is always there for a belt-and-suspenders copy.
