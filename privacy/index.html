Foothold Now

Privacy

What you practice is personal. Foothold Now is built so that as little of it as possible ever leaves your device, and so that what does leave has exactly one job.

The short version: your ladders live in your browser and never leave your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to buy. We don’t collect your data, sell it, share it, or build profiles.

What stays on your device

Everything. Your ladders, steps, ratings, notes, and practice logs are stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device. Nothing you write is sent anywhere at all. The Backup button gives you your data as a file whenever you want it, so you can keep a copy or move it to another device or browser.

What we don’t collect

No names, no profiles, no advertising trackers, no cookies, and no analytics of any kind. Nothing about your visit is measured or sent anywhere, and your ladders are never read for any purpose beyond showing them back to you.

Surveys, only if you opt in

We may occasionally invite feedback—for example, how using the site felt before and after—through an optional form (such as Google Forms), which may be shared as a link on the website. Taking part is entirely your choice, and anything you share there is used only to make Foothold Now better.

Deleting your data

It’s all in your browser, so it’s yours to clear anytime—remove the site’s data in your browser settings, or clear your browser storage, and it’s gone.
