Foothold Now

Email & privacy

What you practice is personal. Foothold Now is built so that as little of it as possible ever leaves your device, and so that what does leave has exactly one job.

The short version: your ladders live in your browser. If you sign in, your email is used for exactly two things—syncing your ladders across your devices, and sending beta users their discount when the subscription launches. That’s the whole list. We don’t sell data, share it, or build profiles.

What stays on your device

Everything, by default. Your ladders, steps, ratings, notes, and practice logs are stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device. If you never sign in, nothing you write is sent anywhere at all. The Backup button gives you your data as a file whenever you want it.

If you sign in with your email

Signing in is optional. If you do, two things are stored on our sync service (Supabase, guarded so that only you can read your own rows):

  • Your email address—used to send your one-time sign-in codes, and, for beta users, to send your 50%-off discount when the subscription launches. Nothing else. No newsletters you didn’t ask for, no marketing lists, never sold or shared.
  • A copy of your ladders—so they can follow you between your phone and computer. That’s the only reason it exists. Signing out stops syncing; your data stays on your device.

What we don’t collect

No names, no profiles, no advertising trackers, no cookies for tracking, no reading of your ladders for any purpose beyond showing them back to you. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for anonymous, cookie-free page counts (how many visits a page gets)—it cannot see who you are or what you write.

Surveys, only if you opt in

During beta we may occasionally invite feedback—for example, how using the site felt before and after—through an optional form (such as Qualtrics or Google Forms). Taking part is entirely your choice, and anything you share there is used only to make Foothold Now better.

Deleting your data
