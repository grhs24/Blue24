Pause

The moment before you react

Something just landed hard.

A message, a look, a silence—and now there's heat in your chest and a story forming fast. This is a short, guided pause built on DBT distress-tolerance skills. Five steps, one at a time, before you do anything you can't take back.

Nothing you type or tap is saved, sent, or seen by anyone. It clears the moment you close the page.

Step 1 · Stop

Stop—just for a moment.

You don't have to figure anything out right now. You only have to not act yet. The moment can wait for you. For the length of this pause, hold off on:

  • sending another message
  • apologizing
  • withdrawing
  • confronting them

None of these are off the table forever. They're just not for right now—not until the wave has had a chance to pass.

Step 2 · Name it

Name what's here.

Naming a feeling loosens its grip a little. Tap whatever fits—feelings and urges both. You can pick more than one.

Step 3 · Rate it

How intense is it, right now?

No wrong answer—just a reading off the dial this second.

0 · barely there10 · overwhelming

Step 4 · Slow down

Let the wave come down.

Follow the circle. Breathe in as it grows, out as it shrinks. Let your jaw unclench, your shoulders drop, your hands go soft. Feelings peak—then they fall. Give this one the time to fall.

Ready

Step 5 · Remember

My feelings are real, but my interpretation hasn't been confirmed yet.

The fear is real. The shame is real. The urge is real. What isn't confirmed is the story—what it meant, what they intended, what happens next. That part is still a guess, and you don't have to act on a guess.

Where you landed

From here you get to choose—wait, check the facts, or respond from steadier ground. Whatever you pick now, you're picking it, not being swept into it.

This is a self-guided prototype for exploring what a DBT tool might feel like—not therapy, and not a crisis service. If you might be in danger or thinking of harming yourself, please reach out to a crisis line or someone you trust right now. In the US you can call or text 988.
