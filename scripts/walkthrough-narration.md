# Foothold Now — Walkthrough Script (dark mode)

Read-along narration for the dark, widescreen walkthrough. Each scene lists the
**on-screen caption** (already burned into the video) and the **line to read**
over it. Total read time ≈ 2.5–3 minutes at a calm pace. Timestamps are
approximate; the video runs 1:38, so leave natural pauses rather than rushing to
keep up with it.

The example ladder is deliberately mild — **getting more comfortable making
phone calls** — so nothing here mirrors anyone's personal or OCD content.

> Tone: warm, unhurried, plain. Let the title cards breathe: start narrating
> once the app appears, and let the closing card sit under your last line.

---

### Title card — open (0:00 – 0:05)
*On screen: the Foothold Now wordmark, the tagline, and the address, on deep green.*

> *(Optional, or leave silent):* "Foothold Now — practice ladders for facing hard things, one small step at a time."

### Scene 1 — Welcome (0:05)
*Caption: "A quiet place to practice the hard things." → "Here's how Foothold Now works."*

> "This is Foothold Now — a quiet place to practice the things you've been putting off or avoiding, one small step at a time. Let me show you how it works."

### Scene 2 — Start a ladder (0:11)
*Caption: "Start a ladder — name what you're working toward." → "A gentle example: getting comfortable on the phone."*

> "Everything centers on a ladder. You tap **Start a ladder** and name what you're working toward. I'll use a gentle example — getting more comfortable making phone calls — and jot down why it matters to me."

### Scene 3 — Add your steps (0:24)
*Caption: "Add small steps in your own words. Rate each 0–100." then, per step, "'…' — NN out of 100."*

> "Now you break it into small steps, written in your own words, and rate each one from **0 to 100** by how hard it feels right now — not how hard it *should* feel. First the easy one: writing down what I want to say. Then calling an automated information line. Then calling a store to ask its hours."

### Scene 4 — Your ladder (0:40)
*Caption: "Your ladder sorts itself — gentlest first, 'Next up' marked."*

> "And there's your ladder — ordered from easiest to hardest, with the gentlest step marked **Next up**, so you always know where to begin."

### Scene 5 — Plan a step with Notes (0:45)
*Caption: "Open a step to plan it." → "Notes has an optional guide: approach, prediction, response to hold back."*

> "Open any step to plan it. Each step has a **Notes** field with optional guide questions — a gentle template: what you'll approach, what you predict might happen, and the response you'll hold back on. You can switch the questions off for a plain note, whichever helps."

### Scene 6 — Log a practice (0:56)
*Caption: "Practiced it? Log how it felt — before, at the peak, after."*

> "When you actually practice a step, you log it: slide how it felt **before**, at its **peak**, and **after**. The 'after' becomes the step's new score — so your ladder quietly reflects your progress."

### Scene 7 — Watch it come down (1:04)
*Caption: "Log a few, and your distress is charted over time." → "Before, peak, and after — watch the numbers come down."*

> "Log a few practices and Foothold Now charts your distress across them — before, peak, and after — so you can actually watch the numbers come down over time."

### Scene 8 — Steps settle (1:14)
*Caption: "When a step loses its grip, mark it settled."*

> "When a step loses its grip — or its 'after' reaches zero — it settles. It stays on your ladder as a marker of how far you've come."

### Scene 9 — Edit or delete (1:19)
*Caption: "The ⋮ menu edits or deletes a ladder — deletions are restorable."*

> "The three-dots menu on any ladder lets you edit its details or delete it. Deleted ladders go to a restorable list, so nothing's ever lost by accident."

### Scene 10 — Private, no account (1:24)
*Caption: "No account, no sign-in. It saves itself in your browser." → "Backup downloads a copy, or moves your ladders to another device."*

> "There's no account and no sign-in. Your ladders save themselves right in your browser and are waiting the next time you come back, and nothing you write is ever sent anywhere. Two things worth knowing: a private or incognito window forgets everything when you close it, and your ladders don't travel between browsers on their own. That's what **Backup** is for — it downloads a copy you can keep, or restore on another device."

### Scene 11 — Guides and starter ladders (1:31)
*Caption: "Guides and ready-made sample ladders live in the menu."*

> "And if you're new to this, the menu has plain-language guides — on building an exposure ladder, on ERP, on the 0-to-100 scale — plus ready-made sample ladders you can add in a single tap and make your own."

### Title card — close (1:34 – 1:38)
*On screen: "Build your ladder. Practice on purpose. Watch the hard things lose their grip." → the wordmark → "Free at grhs24.github.io/foothold-now."*

> "That's Foothold Now — build your ladder, practice on purpose, and watch the hard things slowly lose their grip. It's free, at grhs24 dot github dot io slash foothold-now."
